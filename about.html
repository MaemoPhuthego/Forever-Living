About Forever Living

Rex Maughan — founder of Forever Living, whose vision helped build the brand into a global Aloe Vera business.

Forever Living's brand identity connects Aloe Vera products with wellness, lifestyle support, and personal care.



Our Mission

Since 1978, Forever Living has focused on improving quality of life through Aloe Vera-based wellness, nutrition, and personal care products. The company built its identity on vertically integrated production, meaning it grows, processes, and distributes many of its own Aloe Vera ingredients to maintain consistency and product control.


Built Around Quality

Forever Living emphasizes careful harvesting, inner leaf processing, and product development that blends natural ingredients with practical everyday use. The aim is simple: products that support digestion, immunity, personal care, and general wellbeing without turning the website into a chemistry lecture.


Global Vision

From a single Aloe-based product to a broad international product line, the brand has grown into a globally recognized wellness business. Its reputation has largely been built on brand consistency, distributor networks, and a clear identity centered on Aloe Vera.
